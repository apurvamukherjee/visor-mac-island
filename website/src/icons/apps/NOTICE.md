# Third-party icons

The icons in this folder are trademarks and artwork of their owners: Apple (macOS app and system icons), Google (Chrome, Meet), Meta (WhatsApp), Discord, GitHub, X, Zoom and Anthropic (Claude). They are **not** covered by Visor's GPL-3.0 license and are used only to depict those apps on the website's macOS desktop. Visor is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `*.webp`: exported from the apps installed on a Mac with `swift scripts/mac-icons.swift`, then `cwebp -q 90`.
- `*.svg`: the brand's mark from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (Meet: Google's product logo) on a squircle, built by `node scripts/icons.mjs`.

If you own one of these marks and want it removed, open an issue.
